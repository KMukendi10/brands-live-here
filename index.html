Campaign Overview

A dynamic, multilayered street-level campaign for MTV Base under the Viacom Africa banner. Using township architecture and public walkways as our canvas, we brought culture, youth and storytelling to life through bold, vibrant visuals across Soweto and Maboneng.

  • Visible Culture

    Turn everyday spaces into media moments.

  • Real Connection

    Speak in a language that feels local and authentic.

  • Youth Driven

    Pop culture, social media icons and urban lingo.

  • More Than OOH
